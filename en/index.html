Who we are

About the Initiative

This initiative was launched to strengthen financial literacy across the Arab world, and to shed light on the fraud tactics common in trading, investment, digital currencies, and money transfers. It focuses on awareness, prevention, and sharing knowledge in a clear and direct way.

Our mission

To spread financial and investment awareness and protect individuals from falling victim to fraud.

What the Initiative Covers

Six awareness tracks shape the work of the initiative and guide everything we publish for the public.

Investor Protection

Educational content that helps individuals understand risk before investing.

Fraud Warnings

Highlighting the common methods that fraudsters use.

Digital Awareness

Guidance on protecting personal data and digital wallets.

Awareness Reports

Analytical material that helps make sense of financial-fraud trends.

Public Resources

Simplified information that helps individuals make more informed decisions.

Community Cooperation

Building a culture of reporting, awareness, and financial responsibility.

Our Message

We believe prevention begins with knowledge. The more investors understand fraud tactics, the harder it becomes for fraudsters to exploit them. That is why this initiative publishes awareness content that is clear, practical, and open to the public.

What to do if you face a suspicious situation

Practical steps that help you protect yourself when you suspect a financial dealing.

1

Stop transferring

Do not transfer any further money.

2

Document everything

Keep all conversations and documents.

3

Protect your data

Never share passwords or digital-wallet details.

4

Verify identity

Verify the company identity through official sources.

5

Seek a specialist review

Request a professional assessment if the case needs specialist review.
